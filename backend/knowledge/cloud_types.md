# Cloud Types

## Cumulus
Cumulus clouds are puffy clouds often linked with fair weather, though they can grow taller before showers or thunderstorms.

## Stratus
Stratus clouds form flat gray layers and often create gloomy skies, mist, or light drizzle.

## Nimbus
Nimbus refers to rain-bearing clouds. Nimbostratus clouds usually bring steady widespread rain.

## Cumulonimbus
Cumulonimbus clouds are tall storm clouds associated with thunderstorms, lightning, hail, and severe weather.
