# Farming Weather Advice

## Before Rain
Farmers should secure loose equipment, improve field drainage, delay pesticide spraying, and protect harvested crops from moisture.

## During Heat
Irrigation timing matters. Early morning or evening watering reduces evaporation loss. Livestock also need shade and water.

## Strong Wind
Strong wind can damage weak plants, increase soil moisture loss, and spread fire faster in dry regions.

## Rain Recovery
After heavy rain, farmers should inspect standing water, root damage, fungal disease risk, and nutrient runoff.
