# Weather Terminology

## Rain Probability
Rain probability describes the chance that measurable rain will occur during a forecast period.

## Dew Point
Dew point is the temperature at which air becomes saturated. Higher dew points usually feel more humid and uncomfortable.

## Visibility
Visibility is the distance you can clearly see. Fog, heavy rain, dust, and smoke can reduce it sharply.

## Radar Layer
Radar layers usually show precipitation intensity and movement. They help identify rain bands and storm cells.

## Satellite Layer
Satellite layers show cloud patterns, temperature signatures, and large weather systems over a wide region.
