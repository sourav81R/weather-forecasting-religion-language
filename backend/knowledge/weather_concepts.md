# Weather Concepts

## Humidity and Heat
High humidity makes hot weather feel more intense because sweat evaporates more slowly. This raises the heat index and can increase heat stress.

## Overcast Sky
An overcast sky means clouds cover most or all of the sky. It often reduces direct sunlight and can signal stable cloud cover, drizzle, or a coming rain system depending on pressure and moisture.

## Pressure Changes
Falling air pressure can indicate an approaching storm system. Rising pressure often suggests improving weather.

## UV Index
The UV index estimates how strong ultraviolet radiation is at the surface. Higher UV values increase sunburn risk and make midday outdoor exposure more dangerous.
