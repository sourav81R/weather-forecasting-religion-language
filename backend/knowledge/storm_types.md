# Storm Types

## Thunderstorm
A thunderstorm is a storm with lightning and thunder. It often includes heavy rain, gusty wind, and sometimes hail.

## Severe Thunderstorm
A severe thunderstorm can produce damaging wind, large hail, frequent lightning, and local flooding. It requires extra caution outdoors.

## Cyclone or Hurricane
A cyclone or hurricane is a large rotating storm over warm ocean water. It can bring destructive wind, storm surge, intense rainfall, and prolonged coastal flooding.

## Storm Warning
A warning means dangerous weather is expected soon or is already happening. A watch means conditions are favorable, but the event is not confirmed yet.
