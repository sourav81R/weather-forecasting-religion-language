# Travel Weather Safety

## Rainy Travel
Allow extra travel time, reduce driving speed, and check road visibility. Flash flooding can make roads unsafe even if rain seems moderate.

## Windy Conditions
High-profile vehicles, ferries, and flights are more affected by strong wind. Outdoor sightseeing may also become uncomfortable or risky.

## Heat Safety
Travel in extreme heat requires hydration, rest breaks, lighter clothing, and avoiding peak afternoon sun when possible.

## Storms
When storms are forecast, monitor alerts, avoid open water and exposed ridges, and keep backup indoor plans.
